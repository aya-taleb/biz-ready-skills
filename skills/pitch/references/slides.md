# The slides

Pick 10 to 12. Each slide makes one point. The "needs" column is what must be true and
sourced before the slide can say it.

| slide | must make the investor believe | needs (with its source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | what this is, in one line | business name, the one-line story |
| Problem | a specific person has a costly, frequent problem | the situation (from idea.md), evidence it happens (discovery, reviews in competitors.md) |
| Customer | you know exactly who buys first | the first segment, how many you can reach (market.md bottom-up) |
| Solution | what you do and why it fixes the problem | how it works in 3 steps; a screenshot or photo if one exists |
| Proof | people already want it | test results from validate.md, paying users, retention, real quotes with permission |
| Market | it can get big enough to matter | bottom-up count first (buyers times price), top-down only as a check, each linked |
| Business model | each sale makes money | price, cost per unit, contribution (cfo.md) |
| Competition | you know the rivals and why buyers will pick you | 3 to 5 rivals and substitutes from competitors.md, the gap in one line |
| Go-to-market | you can reach buyers at a cost that works | the channel that tested best, cost per customer, the most you can pay (marketing.md, cfo.md) |
| Team | you are the people to do this | real roles and relevant past work; gaps named with a hiring plan |
| Financials and ask | how much, for what, to reach which milestone | amount, runway it buys, the milestone (cfo.md projections, labeled as projections) |
| Use of funds | the money goes to the riskiest things first | 3 to 4 buckets with amounts that add up to the ask |
| Why now (optional) | something changed that makes this possible now | a dated, linked change (a rule, a cost drop, a new behavior) |

## Order notes

- Strong proof? Move it to slide 3. It is the slide investors repeat to their partners.
- Very early (no proof yet)? Be honest: the proof slide shows the test plan and its pass
  lines from validate.md, and the ask is sized to run those tests.
- Local business pitching a lender or local investor: swap market for "the location" (foot
  traffic or delivery radius, rent, the lease terms) and lean on the business model slide.

## Common investor questions to prepare

- Why will people switch from what they use now?
- What does it cost to get one customer, and how do you know?
- What happens if a big player copies this?
- What is the riskiest thing left, and how does this money remove it?
- Why are you the one to build it?
